# Welcome to Vayu 🌬️

**Vayu** — the autonomous web agent that moves through the internet like wind, finding anything for you.

## What can I do?

Just ask naturally — Vayu figures out where to search, navigates live websites, and returns structured answers with source links.

**Try these examples:**
- ✈️ *"Find flights from Mumbai to Delhi on July 10 under ₹6000"*
- 💰 *"Compare price of iPhone 15 128GB on Amazon and Flipkart"*
- 💼 *"Python ML engineer jobs in Bangalore with 2-4 years experience"*
- 🏆 *"Find open hackathons on Devfolio for AI/ML engineers"*
- 🔍 *"What is the acceptance rate of LeetCode problem 2149?"*
- 📚 *"Find a Django REST framework course under ₹999 on Udemy"*

You can also **upload your resume (PDF)** and ask for job or hackathon matches tailored to your profile.
